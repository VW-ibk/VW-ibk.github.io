About me

I’m from Nigeria and I live in Lappeenranta, Finland, where I study chemical and process engineering at LUT University.

Before engineering, I spent a year studying software engineering. That’s where I learned to program, built my first websites and started trying out AI-assisted coding. I kept building after I switched fields. Now I want to use code for engineering work: calculations, data analysis and models of real processes.

The problems I care about most are in energy, industrial processes and sustainability.
